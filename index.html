About Me

CV

I am building intelligence at Amazon AGI, as an Applied Scientist on Nova vision skill team. I have been working on multi-modal/omni LLMs (Nova2 family) and multimodal embedding model (Nova MME), with a focus on multi-modal data. My specific interests lie in video(+audio), multi-modal agents, and data-centric AI.

I received my Ph.D degree from the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Michigan State University in 2024, advised by Prof. Yu Kong. During Ph.D, I worked on video+language and human action detection. My thesis is Action Modeling in Long-form Videos. I have interned at AWS AI lab and Microsoft Azure.
